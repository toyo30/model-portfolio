# Portfolio assets

Photographs and model details were supplied through the owner's shared [Canva portfolio](https://www.canva.com/design/DAGCjmGiXLM/qrPvxOal2R5AYcRba0Ys7w/view). Images were exported from the rendered ten-page presentation on 2026-09-19 and stored locally, so the website does not depend on expiring Canva media URLs.

`public/images/01.webp` through `19.webp` follow their order of appearance in the presentation. Each has a smaller `-640.webp` responsive version. File 03 is the source's composite contact sheet; individual photographs are used for the website gallery.

Images remain the property of their respective rights holders. This repository does not grant a separate stock-photo license.

The source lists height 162 cm, chest 85 cm, waist 65 cm, hips 92 cm, shoes 38, dark brown eyes and hair, email modelemelinda@gmail.com and Instagram @bisoumelinda. Its age field is intentionally not carried over because it may be out of date. Confirm measurements with the model when arranging a booking.

Site structure reference: [Kim Min portfolio](https://min-kim.netlify.app/). Its images and code were not copied.
